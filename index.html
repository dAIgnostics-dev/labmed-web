LabMed Studio

One platform for everything your lab runs on

LabMed Studio automates the full lab pipeline — from the moment a sample or document arrives to the moment a finished report leaves. Instrument integration, data organization, document validation, scheduling, billing and automated report generation all live in one system, with every result traceable back to its source.

Built for medical, diagnostic and research labs that are tired of stitching together an LIMS, a spreadsheet, a scheduling tool, an invoicing tool and a folder of scripts.

Request a demo

Lab pipeline automation

Connect instruments, import results and metadata automatically, run technical checks and quality control, and push everything through standardized, reproducible workflows.

Data organization and governance

Every measurement stays linked to its sample, operator, instrument, protocol version and analytical pipeline. Versioned, searchable and audit-ready.

Automated document checking

Intake forms, referrals, consent and accompanying documentation are validated for completeness and consistency before they slow anything down.

Work organization and scheduling

Staff shifts, instrument availability, sample queues and turnaround times are planned in one place instead of five.

Billing

Services, panels and cases are tracked from order to invoice, with no manual reconciliation at the end of the month.

Automated report and description generation

Findings, interpretations and result descriptions are drafted from the underlying data, ready for review rather than written from scratch.

Analysis and interpretation

Statistical and AI processing, multimodal integration where relevant, and results presented with confidence, uncertainty and full traceability — never an unexplained score.

  • Standard vocabularies and formats: LOINC, SNOMED CT, DICOM, OMOP
