Solution 01

Health Studio: the complete diagnostics orchestration platform

  • dAI Edge
  • dAI DataHub
  • dAI Inference
  • dAI Clarity

All four layers of the LabMed stack in one product: from e-referral to a digitally signed, structured report delivered into the LIS/EHR.

  • Orchestration of the whole diagnostic flow: e-referral → sample intake → explainable AI analysis → specialist review → signed structured report → delivery to LIS/EHR
  • Explainable AI in every report: saliency views, SHAP feature importance and a ranked differential diagnosis the clinician can audit
  • Structured reports with ICD-10 codes (PDF, FHIR DiagnosticReport, HL7 CDA), a controlled report lifecycle (draft → final → amended) and eIDAS-qualified e-signature
  • Regulatory foundations as functional requirements: EU MDR 2017/745, ISO 13485, GDPR and full audit trails

Specification v1.0 Platform design goals include reducing clinicians' administrative time from 66 % to 33 % of the working day and biopsy processing within 6 hours.

Solution 02

dAI Edge: data sovereignty and local harmonization

  • dAI Edge
  • dAI DataHub

An on-premise layer that connects to everything a hospital already runs — LIS, EHR, PACS, files and streams — and turns heterogeneous data into clean, standardized FHIR R4 records. Raw data never leaves the institution.

  • Multi-source ingestion: FHIR R4 REST, HL7 v2 (MLLP), DICOM, watched files, Kafka/MQTT streams and batch CSV
  • Four-stage validation (syntactic, semantic, referential, business rules) with quarantine and controlled resubmission — no message loss
  • Normalization at the source: SNOMED CT terminology mapping, UCUM unit conversion, MPI patient matching, HL7 v2 → FHIR conversion
  • Append-only audit trail and data preparation for analytics and national exchange

Pilot-ready Security hardening and certified national integration are on the product roadmap.

Solution 03

AI-assisted reporting: from anamnesis to a report draft

  • dAI DataHub
  • dAI Inference
  • dAI Clarity

Semantic search across prior reports and an AI-generated structured report draft — always subject to mandatory physician review, correction and signature.

  • Search by meaning, not keywords: a query for "blood in urine" also finds reports labelled "haematuria"
  • Structured report drafts (diagnosis, ICD-10 code, anamnesis, conclusion) grounded in similar cases from the institution's own archive
  • Runs fully on-premise with local models — no external API keys, data never leaves the clinic
  • A self-improving knowledge base: every corrected and signed report becomes searchable immediately

Prototype A Health Studio module currently in piloting.

Solution 04

National connectivity: CEZIH-ready by design

  • dAI Edge
  • dAI DataHub
  • dAI Clarity

dAIgnostics products are designed for integration with CEZIH — Croatia's national health information system — over HL7 FHIR, with native support for Croatian identifiers and code systems.

  • Reading the national e-record through FHIR R4: encounters, diagnoses (ICD-10), practitioners and organizations
  • Native support for Croatian identifiers: MBO, OIB, HZJZ practitioner number, HZZO organization code
  • Privacy-by-design patient access: lookup only by exact patient ID, a "My patients" panel and a full audit trail of every access
  • Swappable-connector architecture: a certified CEZIH connector plugs in without changing the clinical interface

Sandbox demonstration Demonstrated on realistic test data; certified live integration is in preparation.

Solution 05

AI for hospital operations: resource optimization

  • dAI Inference
  • dAI Clarity

The same approach that reads reports can also optimize how a hospital runs: automated, explainable physician scheduling with guaranteed rule compliance.

  • One-click monthly schedule generation under hard rules: competencies, weekly hour quotas, insurer-contracted slots, travel time between sites, on-call duty
  • Explainability instead of a red cell: every uncovered slot comes with a reason and concrete resolution proposals
  • Automatically ranked substitution proposals for sick leave, justified by competency and hour quotas
  • Versioned schedule publishing, a full audit trail, roles (coordinator, physician, lead, management), print and CSV export

Standalone application Also delivered independently of the LabMed stack.
